Custom Clearance and Trucking Delivery (BEHU Ltd):

Background:
Behu Ltd., a growing company based in Riyadh, specializes in industrial equipment and consumer goods imports. With a significant rise in shipping volumes and complexes with regulations at Saudi Arabia, our client proposed for a partnered operative custom clearance project. Higher volumes of discrepancies and inaccurate documentation have created an unsecured and in-competitive supply chain. Streamlining our client and partnership for an efficient logistic movement, Clarusto Logiatics is ready with its blueprint and execution.

Objectives:
As the client got a referral approach, we endeavor to submit our time-lined strategically coordinated work plan.

·       Ensuring duly and compliant customs clearance.

·       Precisely monitored and tracked consignment avoiding demurrage charges and minimizing delays.

·       Organized filed in documentation for accomplishing regulatory handling and avoiding any withheld.

·       Value added impositions for increasing efficiency in supply chain.

·       Deliberately proactive for maximum risk reduction and consequences.

·       Conformity check guidelines.

·       Satisfactory documentation import check and further proceeding.

·       Inspection and manifestation of HS codes and specification.

·       Discrepancies or distinguished for commercial invoice.

·       Incomplete paperwork.
