
Empty Plastic Bottle Supply from Netherlands to UK
Background:This study is based for the consignment of empty plastic bottles from EU to UK being referred the client from Clarusto Logistics trusted Client and partner. The client is engaged into plastic recycle undertaking in Worcester (UK) and so is looking for the supply of recycled empty plastic bottles with a palletized weight of 3500-3800 kilograms, counting 70 pallets in a single delivery. Clarusto’s experienced team and efficient networking conducted a survey within UK and EU and hence finalized the most compatible option from EU and processed the entire logistic further to continue and grow promising and sustainable company profile.
/

Consented deadlines to be met within 3-5 days oof duration.
Optimized transportation cost efficiency.
Effective border and custom clearance for end-to-end planned delivery.
Strategic load optimized pallet transition for cubic efficiency.
Concerned to avoid any deformation though shrinking.
Prevention against plastic consignment to delivery in original form.
Pre analysis of routing and transit surveys.

Maximizing vertical stacking.
All declaration authorized documentation including export-import fulfilling norms.
