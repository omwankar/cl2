Heavy Industrial Cargo Transportation from China to 
Saudi Arabia 
 
Overview
 
Clarusto Logistics successfully managed the end-to-end transportation of 130 Axial 
Metal Expansion Joints from China to Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, for a leading construction 
sector client. Following the successful execution of previous shipments, the client once 
again entrusted Clarusto with the transportation of this critical industrial cargo. 
The shipment comprised 124 units of Model P3200 and 6 units of Model P1200, 
transported in 20FT and 40FT Full Container Load (FCL) containers, with each 
container weighing approximately 22 tonnes. The scope of work included export 
coordination, ocean freight, customs clearance, port handling, documentation 
management, and final inland transportation to the project site in Riyadh. 
Through meticulous planning and seamless coordination, Clarusto ensured the cargo 
was delivered safely, efficiently, and within the required project timeline. 
 
The Challenge 
Transporting heavy industrial components across international borders required careful 
planning, strict regulatory compliance, and close coordination between multiple

stakeholders. The weight and dimensions of the cargo demanded optimized container

Key challenges included: 
• Managing containers weighing approximately 22 tonnes while complying with

• Selecting the appropriate 20FT and 40FT FCL containers for heavy cargo

• Ensuring compliance with SOLAS Verified Gross Mass (VGM) regulations.

• Managing potential congestion at the origin port without affecting vessel

• Preventing overweight penalties, storage charges, demurrage, detention, and

• Maintaining seamless coordination between ocean freight and inland 
transportation to Riyadh.  
 
Clarusto Logistics' Approach 
Clarusto Logistics developed a comprehensive logistics strategy to ensure the 
shipment moved efficiently from origin to final destination while maintaining full 
compliance with international shipping and Saudi import regulations. 
The logistics solution included: 
• Detailed assessment of cargo dimensions, weight, and handling requirements.  
• Selection of the most suitable 20FT and 40FT FCL container configurations.  
• Optimized container loading to maximize space utilization while remaining 
within permissible weight limits.  
• Compliance with SOLAS Verified Gross Mass (VGM) requirements.  
• Preparation and verification of all export, shipping, and customs documentation.  
• Coordination with shipping lines, port authorities, customs officials, and inland 
transport providers.  
• Continuous shipment monitoring and proactive communication throughout the 
transport cycle.

Execution
 
Prior to shipment, Clarusto Logistics conducted a detailed cargo assessment and 
developed an optimized container loading plan to ensure safe transportation and 
compliance with all applicable regulations. 
All export documentation, customs declarations, and shipping documents were 
prepared and verified before vessel departure from China. Continuous coordination 
with shipping lines and port authorities ensured timely vessel bookings and smooth 
cargo handling despite operational constraints. 
Upon arrival in Saudi Arabia, customs clearance and port handling were completed 
efficiently before the cargo was transferred for inland transportation. Dedicated 
transport resources and continuous operational monitoring enabled rapid delivery to 
the project site in Riyadh, while ensuring cargo integrity throughout the journey. 
Close coordination between shipping partners, customs authorities, transport 
providers, and the client ensured complete shipment visibility and uninterrupted 
execution from origin to destination.

The project was completed successfully, with all 130 Axial Metal Expansion Joints
